## 5. Decision (5 min)

> Each person states their recommendation aloud, in this order: most skeptical first, most enthusiastic last. Hiring manager goes last.

| Interviewer | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| R1 | ☐ Strong No Hire  ☐ No Hire  ☐ Hire  ☐ Strong Hire |
| R2 | ☐ Strong No Hire  ☐ No Hire  ☐ Hire  ☐ Strong Hire |
| R3 | ☐ Strong No Hire  ☐ No Hire  ☐ Hire  ☐ Strong Hire |
| R4 | ☐ Strong No Hire  ☐ No Hire  ☐ Hire  ☐ Strong Hire |

**Group decision:**

- ☐ **Strong Hire** — Extend offer today
- ☐ **Hire** — Extend offer this week
- ☐ **No Hire** — Pass on this candidate
- ☐ **Strong No Hire** — Pass and don't revisit
- ☐ **Lean Hire** — Defer; gather one more signal (specify what: ___)
- ☐ **Lean No Hire** — Defer; not enough signal against (specify what: ___)

**If split decision:** the tiebreaker is the *risk* answer in section 4, not seniority of interviewer. The hiring manager makes the final call.

---

## 6. Logistics (5 min)

- [ ] Recruiter to communicate decision to candidate within 24h
- [ ] If hire: hiring manager writes the offer rationale (for the candidate)
- [ ] If no hire: interviewer assigned to write the rejection note (1 sentence, kind, specific)
- [ ] Scorecards filed in ATS / shared drive for future reference
- [ ] Note any improvements to the interview kit for next time

---

## Decision Heuristics (use sparingly)

These are tiebreakers, not rules:

- **All 3s, no 4s, no 2s** → Hire. A solid bar-meeting candidate is a hire.
- **One 2, rest 3s** → Discuss the 2 specifically. A single weak competency may be teachable; a single weak competency in the *core* of the role is a no.
- **All 4s** → Strong hire, move fast.
- **Spread on P2 (LLM/Agent)** → This is the differentiating competency. Take disagreement on P2 more seriously than disagreement on P1.
- **High P5, low elsewhere** → Likely a great collaborator but not yet at the technical bar. No hire for this role, possibly revisit in 12 months.
- **Low P5, high elsewhere** → Strong IC, weak teammate. Discuss with hiring manager whether the role can absorb that.
- **Strong opinions, weak evidence** → Probe the evidence in section 2. "I just didn't like them" is not a signal.
